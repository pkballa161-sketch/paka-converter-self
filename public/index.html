Questions

Is there a limit on video length?

Hours-long videos are fine. Very long ones simply take longer to prepare, and live streams work once they have ended.

What quality do I get?

Audio is genuinely re-encoded, so you can take a video and get MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus or Vorbis — a real FLAC, not a renamed MP3. Video is MP4 from 144p up to 4K or 8K where the upload has it, or MOV. MKV isn't one of the containers the service builds, so it isn't offered.

Which platforms work?

YouTube (including Shorts and live), TikTok, Instagram posts and reels, Facebook videos and X posts. Private, age-restricted or login-only posts can't be fetched by any tool.

Where do my files go?

They are not stored, indexed or shared. A converted file lives on the server just long enough for your browser to download it, then it is deleted.

Can I download anything?

Only content you own or have permission to use — your own uploads, Creative Commons material, or anything the rights holder allows. Respect YouTube's terms and local copyright law.

Run it yourself

This edition has no credits and no API key. It talks to a converter you control — yt-dlp and ffmpeg on a machine you own — which is why it can genuinely build FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, MOV and more, with nothing to pay per download.
